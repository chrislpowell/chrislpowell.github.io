Standing in Reality

Standing in Reality is an ongoing philosophical project concerned with how individuals relate to meaning, identity, and experience.

This site offers a brief introduction to the work, without instruction or expectation.

The material here is offered as a description of structure, not a prescription for how to live or think.

The note on reading at your own pace offers a way to approach the work.
